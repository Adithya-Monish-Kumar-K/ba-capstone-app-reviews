Generated by `scripts/24_dataset_documentation.py`; numbers in `data/dataset_validation.json`.

## Known data issues (kept and flagged, not hidden)

| Issue | Handling |
|---|---|
| Feed gap 21 Apr – 5 May for Swiggy, Blinkit, Domino's, Flipkart and Amazon: positive reviews drop 59–90%, negative reviews far less | Flagged (`feed_gap`); time series use counts and pass the gap to ARIMA as an input; share-based trends exclude the window |
| Zomato has no reviews 23 Jul 22:00 – 25 Jul 12:30 (source outage, re-query returned the same) | 24 Jul filled for forecasting only (`neg_reviews_filled`) |
| `app_version` empty for 13.4% of reviews | Kept; not a model input; release events use the rest |
| Median review is 2 words | Kept; text mining uses reviews with ≥ 3 content words |
